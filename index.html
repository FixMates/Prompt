Made for makers

Creative, local, and straightforward.

Prompt Master is an independent browser tool. Your prompt, logo, and photos stay on your device. It is not affiliated with Instagram, Meta, OpenAI, or any image-generation provider.

◈

Your privacy

Prompts, images, logo, and music are processed in your browser and are not uploaded. Project settings are stored locally only when you choose Save project.

♫

Music licensing

No soundtrack is bundled. Only use music you own or are licensed to publish.

✦

About the export

The same timeline renderer draws the preview and exported video. Optional FFmpeg WebAssembly converts the recording to genuine H.264 MP4 locally.

A few answers

Frequently asked questions.

Does Prompt Master upload my images?

No. Images, prompts, logo, and music stay in your browser. MP4 encoding uses the included local FFmpeg core.

Can I export an MP4?

Supported browsers record the composed 9:16 storyboard to WebM and locally transcode it to H.264 MP4. WebM is also available.

Can I include music?

Trim a local track, adjust volume and fades, and enable Mix into video. Publish music only when you have permission.

What gets saved?

Project settings and a small uploaded logo may be saved locally. Original and generated photos and music must be selected again after reload.
