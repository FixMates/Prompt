Made for makers

Creative, local, and straightforward.

Prompt Master is an independent browser tool. Your prompt and image stay on your device. It is not affiliated with Instagram, Meta, OpenAI, or any image-generation provider.

◈

Your privacy

Prompts, images, and music are processed in your browser and are not uploaded. Lightweight settings are saved locally only when you choose Save project. Clearing browser data can remove them.

♫

Music licensing

No music is bundled. Only use music you own or have permission to publish. User-selected local music can be previewed and mixed into video exports.

✦

About the export

Canvas renders the composed storyboard, then MediaRecorder captures it. Optional FFmpeg WebAssembly converts it to genuine H.264 MP4 locally.

A few answers

Frequently asked questions.

Does Prompt Master upload my image?

No. Image, prompt, and music processing happens locally. The pinned FFmpeg core (~32 MB) is included with this app and loaded only when MP4 is requested. Your media stays local. Google Fonts may also be requested.

Can I export an MP4?

Supported browsers can record the storyboard to WebM and locally transcode it with FFmpeg WebAssembly to H.264 MP4. This can be slow and memory intensive; WebM is available as a faster fallback.

Can I include music?

Select a local track, preview it, adjust volume and fades, and enable Mix into video. Use music you own or are licensed to publish.

What gets saved?

Save project stores settings and editable text locally. Images and music are not stored and must be selected again after reload.
